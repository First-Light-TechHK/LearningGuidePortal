Goodman-Bacon 2×2: Treat × Post, not 15 per cent alone

Worked numbers. Suppose the treated series falls 15 percentage points after the charge, and the untreated series falls 4 points over the same window. Treated change = −15 pp, comparison change = −4 pp, so β̂2×2 = −15 − (−4) = −11 pp. The public 15 per cent, with no comparison group, is not this estimator. Incidence is not in that average.

Contrast cases — the difference of two changes

Case 1 — the 2×2. Treated change = −15 pp, comparison change = −4 pp. Then β̂2×2 = −15 − (−4) = −11 pp.
Case 2 — the public number. “Traffic fell 15 per cent” with no untreated series is not this estimator. The solid drop alone is not the DD.
Case 3 — what is not in the average. Incidence (who pays) is not in that −11 pp. An 11-point contrast is not “congestion charging works”.
