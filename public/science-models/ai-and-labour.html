Acemoglu & Restrepo Fig. 1: I moves; N is observed

Worked numbers. Tasks live on an interval of length 1. Start with I = 0.35 (capital on the left), labour on (I, N]. Move I to 0.55: automation takes tasks; that is not yet a labour-share forecast. N stays at 1.00 until you extend it. Extending N is an observation of new labour-intensive tasks, not a default from 1820. Cobb–Douglas with a frozen sL = 0.60 cannot ask this question.

Contrast cases — I moves; N is observed

Case 1 — automation only. Start at I = 0.35. Move I to 0.55. Capital takes tasks on the left. That displacement is not yet a labour-share forecast.
Case 2 — new tasks. N stays at 1.00 until you extend it. Extending N is an observation of new labour-intensive tasks, not a default from 1820.
Case 3 — three objects. Hours, the wage, and sL = wL/Y are not the same thing. Cobb–Douglas with a frozen sL = 0.60 cannot ask this question.
