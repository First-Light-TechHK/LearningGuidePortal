Bertozzi Eq. (4): stocks, not the news curve

Worked numbers. Set R₀ = β/γ = 3 and γ = 1. Then HIT = 1 − 1/R₀ = 2/3. The infection peak on Figure 2 sits at s = 1/R₀ = 1/3. A falling reported case-count is not “the virus has weakened”, and it is not the stock I.
