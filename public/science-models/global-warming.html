Jeevanjee: forcing is not a temperature

Worked numbers. Take the paper’s comprehensive doubling forcing F2x = 4 W m−2 and a feedback κ = 2 W m−2 K−1. Then ΔTs = F/κ = 2.0 K. That 2 K is an equilibrium change. A local summer of +1.2 K is a weather point, not ECS.

Contrast cases — same equation, different objects

Case 1 — paper default. F2x = 4 W m−2, κ = 2 W m−2 K−1. Then ΔTs = F/κ = 2.0 K. That is an equilibrium change after the feedback has acted.
Case 2 — a weather point. A local summer of +1.2 K is a tick on the ΔT bar, not ECS. Do not read the dashed summer line as the forcing or as the equilibrium.
Case 3 — a wrong reading. F is not a temperature. A no-feedback first-year transient is not “the warming we shall see”.
