# Portal Ant Design requirements

User approved the four-stage plan on 2026-10-28.

1. When a learner opens Portal, authentication, Contact or Subscription, controls shall use a learner-specific theme while preserving the approved Figma layout, assets, typography and bilingual copy.
2. When an action submits, the UI shall retain native validation, show its pending state, prevent competing submissions and permit retry after failure. Navigation links shall retain their existing destinations and returnTo behavior.
3. When selecting a plan, the UI shall preserve service-provided eligibility, period/category selection, displayed prices and card selection styling.
4. When using the account menu or specialised confirmation dialogs, keyboard access, dismissal and focus shall remain usable on mobile and desktop.
5. Migration shall retain all service/API/database/payment/entitlement contracts and isolate Backoffice styling. Exact Figma and target-environment acceptance must be reported separately from local verification.
