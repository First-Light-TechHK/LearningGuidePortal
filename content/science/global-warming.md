## What the public word fuses

“Global warming” is not one quantity. Keep apart:

- forcing $F$ (W m$^{-2}$), the drop in outgoing longwave at fixed temperature
- the feedback parameter $\kappa$ (W m$^{-2}$ K$^{-1}$)
- the temperature change $\Delta T$

A local summer, or a news-line through two annual temperatures, is not climate sensitivity.

## Governing form

Planetary energy balance, after Jeevanjee’s chalkboard notes and the IPCC AR6 energy-budget language:

$$
C\frac{dT}{dt}=F-\kappa\Delta T
$$

In equilibrium the storage term is zero, so

$$
\Delta T=\frac{F}{\kappa},\qquad \mathrm{ECS}=\frac{F_{2x}}{\kappa}
$$

$F_{2x}$ is the forcing from a sustained doubling of CO$_2$, of order $4\,\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}$. ECS is that equilibrium $\Delta T$, not a weather.

If the student offers $2.2\,\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}$ as “the warming”, type-check: that is a forcing, not a temperature.

## Refused poor form

Refuse a fitted straight line through two annual temperatures. Refuse Newton cooling of a room as the planetary form. Do not invent $288\,\mathrm{K}$ or an albedo of $0.3$ unless the student named them as a stated standard.

## Fence

A no-feedback rise is not “the warming we shall see”. A single season at one station is not an ECS estimate.

## When the student fails

- Fuses the public word: name $F$, $\kappa$, and $\Delta T$. Do not calculate yet.
- Fits a news-line: name the refused form; load forcing–feedback.
- Inserts $288\,\mathrm{K}$ or albedo silently: stop.
- Reports W m$^{-2}$ as degrees: type-check.
- Treats the early transient as balance and never marks the forcing arrow: point to the arrow.
- Closes with the newspaper sentence: fence.
