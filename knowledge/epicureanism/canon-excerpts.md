# Epicureanism Canon Excerpts For Demo

Purpose: small primary-text set for the AI Tutor demo. This is not a full source edition.

## Primary Canon Used Now

### 1. Epicurus, Letter to Menoeceus

Edition used in demo: Robert Drew Hicks translation, Internet Classics Archive.

Source URL: https://classics.mit.edu/Epicurus/menoec.html

Why it matters:

- Best compact source for Epicurus' ethics.
- Defines pleasure, desire, fear, prudence, and the aim of life.
- Directly answers the common student question: "Does pleasure mean indulgence?"

Demo anchors:

- pleasure as freedom from pain and fear
- desire classification
- not choosing every pleasure
- simple living
- prudence

### 2. Epicurus, Principal Doctrines

Edition used in demo: Robert Drew Hicks translation, Internet Classics Archive.

Source URL: https://classics.mit.edu/Epicurus/princdoc.html

Why it matters:

- Concise doctrinal statements.
- Useful for answer checking and rubric design.
- Adds the limit of pleasure, friendship, security, justice, and desire categories.

Demo anchors:

- limit of pleasure
- friendship
- natural and unnecessary desires
- justice and security
- fear and disturbance

## How The Demo Uses The Canon

The source text is not used as a generic document dump. It is mapped into teaching controls:

| Canon point | Teaching control |
|---|---|
| Pleasure aims at freedom from pain/fear | Corrects "pleasure = indulgence" |
| Not every pleasure should be chosen | Explains judgement and prudence |
| Pleasure has a limit once want is removed | Explains why luxury is not the goal |
| Friendship is central to happiness | Corrects "Epicureanism = selfishness" |
